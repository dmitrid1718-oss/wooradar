THE UNWANTED NEWS RADAR

What gets ignored, reshaped, or removed

A source-led desk for stories with little broad coverage, powerful gatekeepers in the record, public narratives that shift, and pages that vanish soon after publication.

COVERAGE GAPS

Signals outside the usual news cycle

Find events in primary records, technical status pages, trade reporting, and local notices. Compare a consistent set of broad outlets before calling a story undercovered.

INFORMATION GATEKEEPERS

Who shaped access or distribution?

Track documented platform or government actions affecting search, ranking, moderation, access, or reach. Identify the actor, mechanism, timeline, and supporting record.

FRAMING / DISAPPEARANCE

What changed, and when?

Preserve first-seen copies and timestamps when stories are revised or removed. Distinguish corrections, withdrawals, paywalls, broken links, and unexplained disappearance.

AI / ESSENTIAL SYSTEMS

Failures that rarely become headlines

Include AI model, API, sign-in, and service interruptions from provider status histories, alongside cyber incidents affecting essential systems.

Low coverage, company involvement, a changed narrative, or a vanished page is a lead to investigate—not proof of suppression. Each entry will separate confirmed facts, allegations, and interpretation, with primary sources and competing explanations.

METHOD / LIMITS

Small signals. No invented certainty.

We pull public headlines from a news RSS source and link to the source pages. Automated selection can miss events, repeat coverage, or include weak reporting. Dates, publication links and the last-update time remain visible. This is a reading desk, not an original reporting service.
