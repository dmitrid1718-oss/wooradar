Evidence rules

The instrument distinguishes NASA fireball observations, civilian UAP claims, GEIPAN case records, official UAP document catalogs, NOAA planetary Kp readings, NASA DONKI solar events, and arXiv preprints. Unlocated scientific records appear only in the feed. External reports are neither automatically verified nor proof of extraordinary phenomena. NUFORC factual fields are credited via The UAP Atlas, with original narratives left at NUFORC. Source refresh timestamps do not indicate event times. Individual connectors may fail without affecting other feeds.
